# Story map

| Question | Dashboard evidence | Notebook evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Do everyday routines differ? | Select a routine; compare actual typical values; inspect distributions when needed | Five routine boxplots, counts, averages, typical values, and spread |
| What do money and background add? | Savings, investment, or housing per $100 of income; wealth shares by income, age, education, or saving group | Financial boxplots, group bars, scatter, and correlation analysis |
| Does the pattern remain with similar backgrounds? | Select a life stage; compare both wealth groups within fixed income bands | All 16 age-and-income comparisons, eligible counts, and a savings comparison for each age band |

Main charts show actual values or millionaires per 100 profiles. The word “typical” means the middle recorded value. Unknown reading values remain blank. Context bands keep the full-file definitions. Unsupported small comparisons are withheld rather than replaced with full-file results.
